# Rhythm Music Licenses

## CC0 rhythm tracks

The 14 files named `CC0_*.m4a` are converted from the following OpenGameArt
releases by **Tricks & Traps**:

- [Free Rhythm Game Music Pack 1](https://opengameart.org/content/free-rhythm-game-music-pack-1)
- [Free Rhythm Game Music Pack 2](https://opengameart.org/content/free-rhythm-game-music-pack-2)

Both source pages identify the music as **CC0 1.0 Universal**. CC0 permits
copying, modification, distribution, performance, and commercial use without
requesting permission. The license does not provide a warranty or affect any
unrelated trademark, publicity, or privacy rights.

- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Creator credit: Tricks & Traps
- Retrieved: 2026-07-11
- Attribution: not required by CC0; retained here as good provenance practice

Per-file source URLs and SHA-256 hashes for both the downloaded WAV files and
the bundled AAC conversions are recorded in
`Licenses/cc0_rhythm_tracks.json`.

`Rhythm_Tutorial.m4a` is a 67.2-second excerpt derived from the bundled CC0
track `CC0_10_Extended.m4a`. It is used only by the repeatable beginner
tutorial. Its derivation and bundled SHA-256 are recorded in the same ledger.
